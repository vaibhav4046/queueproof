- Filename: `queueproof-demo-voice-v2.wav`
- WAV PCM, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono
- Dry voice only; no music, reverb, ambience, denoise pumping, limiter, or baked loudness effect
- 0.5 seconds clean silence at start/end

Generate or record the narration as one continuous file. Send the untouched master. The editor will
split on the silent gaps, align it to the Supademo steps, apply only light cleanup, and add optional
original/CC0 instrumental music at least 18 dB below the narration.
