# QueueProof — judge one-pager

## The gap

Agents are increasingly capable of execution, but work evidence is fragmented and priority claims are rarely reproducible. QueueProof is the control plane between enterprise knowledge and agent execution.

## The mechanism

HydraDB supplies dynamic connector contracts and grounded retrieval. QueueProof proves connection state with sync evidence and provider-matched canary results, routes each question, scores candidate work with a versioned deterministic policy, and emits a Proof Packet or bounded Execution Packet.

## The safety boundary

Source text is untrusted evidence. Credentials are encrypted. MCP is authenticated and workspace-bound. Writes are proposals until a human approves an evidence-linked, risk-classified, idempotent action. Fixture mode cannot silently appear in production.

## Current evidence

The local codebase has passing type, ranking, retrieval, security, contracts, MCP, and configuration-preservation checks. Live connector, hosted MCP, and cross-source evaluation proof remain pending external authorisation and are labelled accordingly.
