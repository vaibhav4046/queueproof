# Commitment audit

List commitments and untracked commitments. Classify explicit, implied, satisfied, overdue, superseded, and conflicted only when evidence supports the label.

Required tools: `queueproof_get_commitments`, `queueproof_get_untracked_commitments`, `queueproof_list_conflicts`.
Permissions: read commitments and cited sources. No write permission.
